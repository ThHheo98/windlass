# Windlass v1.0 scope

## In

- Navigable waterways from the Iver/Uxbridge area, including the Slough Arm and Packet Boat Marina.
- The Grand Union Canal through London, including Paddington Basin and Little Venice.
- Regent's Canal, including Camden Locks and the Mile End area.
- Limehouse Basin and the Limehouse Cut.
- The Lee Navigation from Bow Locks north to Pickett's (Alfie's) Lock.

## Out

- The tidal Thames, Bow Creek, and Prescott Channel.
- Bow Back Rivers and Olympic Park waterways that are not part of the Lee Navigation route.
- Other waterways inside the region polygon that are not part of the connected route above.

## Success test

- The region GeoJSON covers the in-scope waterways and endpoint locks.
- The OSM pipeline can fetch and connect mapped waterways along the route from Packet Boat Marina to Pickett's (Alfie's) Lock.
