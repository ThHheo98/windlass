# Windlass v1.0 scope

## In

- Grand Union main line from Uxbridge Lock to Bulls Bridge Junction.
- Slough Arm from Cowley Peachey Junction to Slough Basin, including Packet Boat Marina.
- Paddington Arm from Bulls Bridge to Little Venice, including Paddington Basin.
- Regent's Canal from Little Venice to Limehouse Basin, including Camden Locks and the Mile End area.
- Hertford Union Canal from Regent's Canal at Victoria Park to the Lee at Old Ford.
- Limehouse Cut from Limehouse Basin to Bow Locks.
- Lee Navigation from Bow Locks north to Pickett's (Alfie's) Lock.

## Out

- The tidal Thames, Bow Creek, and Prescott Channel.
- Bow Back Rivers and Olympic Park waterways that are not listed above, including branches off the Lee Navigation route.
- Other waterways inside the region polygon that are not part of the connected route above.

## Success test

- The region GeoJSON covers every listed section, junction, basin, and endpoint lock from Uxbridge Lock and Slough Basin to Pickett's (Alfie's) Lock.
- The OSM pipeline can fetch and connect the listed waterways from the Slough Arm and Grand Union through the Paddington and Regent's canals, Hertford Union, Limehouse Cut, and Lee Navigation.
